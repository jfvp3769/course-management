Portal Walkthrough

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

1. Institution Branding & Semester Dates

Click Semester Settings in the header. Set your School or Department Name, upload your institutional crest or logo, and configure your Term Title, Start Date, and End Date. The engine automatically lays out your complete 18-week semester calendar.

2. Academic Calendar Synchronization

Navigate to Academic Calendar Events (Tab 3) and click Upload / Replace Calendar → Load Sample AY 2026–2027 Calendar to instantly load verified midterm/final exam periods and institutional holidays, or import custom CSV/PDF syllabus dates.

Course Management & Sections

Open Manage Subjects from the top bar to create Course Codes (e.g., CVE111), Subject Titles, and assign color themes. Under each course, click + Add Section to configure section codes (e.g., B15.1), meeting days, class times, and room assignments.

Timetable & Conflict Auditor

Switch to Weekly Timetable (Tab 2) to view your visual schedule. The built-in Conflict Auditor automatically detects room collisions or overlapping section times in real time, alerting you with direct links to resolve conflicts.

Spreadsheet Enrollment & Student Records

Go to Class List (Roster) (Tab 4), select your section, and click Paste Class List. Copy columns directly from Excel or Google Sheets (ID, Last Name, First Name, Email) to enroll entire classes in seconds.

Integrated Faculty Communications

Use the roster sidebar tools to trigger 1-click individual emails, filtered group notifications, or section BCC broadcasts via your desktop client or configured Google Workspace account.

18-Week Lesson Matrix

In the Lesson Planner Matrix (Tab 1), plan topics and activity types (Lecture, Lab, Quiz, Exam) across all 18 weeks. Filter by all courses, single subject, or individual section to track your syllabus delivery with drag-and-drop flexibility.

Automatic Schedule Shifting

When unexpected weather or institutional suspensions occur, open the slot and click 🚫 Mark "No Class" & Push Forward. All subsequent planned activities automatically shift forward by one meeting slot, preserving your chronological lesson plan.

Dynamic Criteria & Per-Subject Scales

In Class Record & Grades (Tab 5), click Grading Criteria to customize category weights (Quizzes, Exams, Labs). Click Configure Grading Scale to set grade equivalence thresholds globally, per Subject (e.g. CVE111), or override per section.

Real-Time Computations & Radar Filters

Grades calculate automatically upon score entry (1.00–5.00). Click the At-Risk Student Radar or Input Error Radar badge pills in the header to instantly isolate students who need remediation or entries that require score corrections.

Offline Local Storage & Privacy

All grades, rosters, schedules, and configurations are stored 100% locally on your device. No student records are ever transmitted over external servers or third-party clouds.

Export / Import Hub & Automated Backups

Open Export / Import Hub to download complete JSON backups or export spreadsheet-ready CSV files for Class Records, Rosters, and Schedules. Built-in reminders prompt you regularly to safeguard your work.
