Portal Walkthrough

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

1. Configure Semester Dates

Click Semester Settings in the header. Set your Term Title, Start Date, and End Date. The engine automatically creates your entire 18-week grid and date rows.

2. Institutional Calendar

Go to Academic Calendar Events (Tab 3) and click Upload / Replace Calendar → Load Official MSU-GSC AY 2026–2027 Calendar to sync verified examination periods and holidays.

Courses, Sections & Meeting Hours

Open Manage Subjects from the top bar. Enter Course Code (e.g., CVE112), Title, and select a color theme.

Under each course, click + Add Section to configure section codes (e.g., E15.1), meeting days, class hours, and room assignments. These synchronize immediately with your Weekly Timetable.

Student Roster Enrollment

Go to Class List (Roster) (Tab 4), choose your target section, and click Paste Class List.

Copy your student columns directly from Excel or Google Sheets (ID, Last Name, First Name, Email) and paste them in. You can also broadcast emails to the active section from the sidebar.

Lesson Pacing & Schedule Shifting

In the Lesson Planner Matrix (Tab 1), click any empty slot (+ Click to plan) to log topics and activity types (Lecture, Lab, Quiz, Exam).

If a class is suspended, open the slot and click 🚫 Mark "No Class" & Push Forward. All subsequent planned activities automatically shift forward by one meeting slot, preserving your syllabus sequence.

Real-Time Weighted Grading

In Class Record & Grades (Tab 5), click Grading Criteria to define categories and weight percentages (e.g., Quizzes 20%, Exams 20%). Weights must sum to 100%.

Enter student marks directly in the grid using arrow keys or Enter. The engine computes weighted totals, official MSU grades (1.00–5.00), and flags students on the At-Risk Student Radar.

Offline Local Storage & Backups

All data automatically saves to your browser's local storage.

To migrate data to another computer or safeguard your work, open Export / Import Hub → Complete Backup → Download Backup JSON. You can restore this file anytime.
